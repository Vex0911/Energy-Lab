Rebirth

0 RP

Rebirthing resets energy and upgrades. Each rebirth gives +20% to everything and Rebirth Points. You need rebirths to ascend, and ascending resets them.

Rebirth Shop

Rebirth once to unlock the shop.

Shop levels can be sold back for 90% of what you paid. Pulse Accelerator shortens the time between automatic Energy payouts.

Ascension

0 AP

Current tier: Core · Next tier: Stellar at 5 ascensions.

Ascending resets your rebirths, Rebirth Points, energy, upgrades and rebirth shop levels, except rebirth upgrades you have fully maxed, which are kept. In return you get Ascension Points and a permanent multiplier.

Ascension Shop

Ascend once to unlock the shop.

Shop levels can be sold back for 90% of what you paid.

Challenges

Ascend once to unlock challenges.

Starting a challenge resets your run like an ascension (no Ascension Points). Reach the ascension requirement under the challenge rules, then complete it for a permanent reward. Three random challenges are offered at a time. Rerolling the offers costs energy.
